Testing

What to know about the SAT, ACT, PSAT, and AP exams.

SAT

A core part of most college applications. Start with a practice test to see where you stand, then focus your studying on your weakest section instead of reviewing everything equally.

ACT

Covers similar ground to the SAT but includes a science section and moves faster. Take a practice test for each to see which format fits you better before committing to one.

PSAT

A lower-stakes practice run for the SAT, usually taken sophomore or junior year. A strong score can also qualify you for National Merit recognition, so it's worth taking seriously.

APs

Give you a shot at college credit and show colleges you can handle rigor. Pick ones tied to your interests or strengths, and pace your studying across the year instead of cramming before the exam.
