Clubs

Find your people, build real skills, and get involved outside the classroom.

Why join a club?

Clubs are one of the easiest ways to find people who share your interests, build real skills, and show colleges what you care about outside of class.

Finding the right fit

Academic, cultural, service, and hobby clubs all offer something different. Try a few during the first weeks of school before committing to one long-term.

Starting your own club

Can't find a club for what you're into? Most schools let students start their own with a staff advisor, a short proposal, and a handful of interested members.

Taking on leadership

Running for an officer position is a great way to grow beyond just being a member. Start by showing up consistently and volunteering for small tasks.
