College Prep

Get ahead on applications, testing, and everything in between.

Building your list

Start researching schools early and build a balanced list of reach, target, and safety schools based on your grades, interests, and budget.

Testing and applications

Know the deadlines for standardized tests, essays, and applications well in advance. Give yourself time to write multiple drafts of your essays instead of rushing at the last minute.

Letters of recommendation

Ask teachers and counselors who know you well, and give them plenty of notice, along with a resume or brag sheet to help them write a strong letter.

Financial aid and scholarships

Fill out the FAFSA and CSS Profile as soon as they open, and keep a running list of scholarships you qualify for well before senior year deadlines hit.
