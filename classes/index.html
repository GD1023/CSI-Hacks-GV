Navigating Classes

A quick guide to building a schedule that actually works for you.

Choosing your schedule

Picking classes each year can feel overwhelming. Think about your interests, your workload, and how each class fits into your long-term goals before you sign up.

AP, Honors, and regular classes

More advanced classes can boost your GPA and look good on applications, but only take on what you can handle without burning out. Balance is key.

Talking to your counselor

Your school counselor can help you plan out your four years, catch graduation requirement gaps, and suggest classes you might not have considered.

Managing your workload

A schedule full of hard classes and heavy extracurriculars can burn you out fast. Leave room to breathe, especially if it's your first time taking a harder course load.
